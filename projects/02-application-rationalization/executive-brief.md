# Executive Brief: Meridian Application Rationalization

## Decision requested

Approve a bounded discovery and candidate-assessment phase.
Do not approve application retirement or a consolidation target yet.

This is a simulated decision request for a fictional company.

## Business problem

Meridian operates separate property registers and lease applications
for its North and South entities.

The landscape contains different billing handoff mechanisms and assumed
retail spreadsheet calculations. These create questions about support
effort, data consistency and recovery that require assessment.

No measured business loss or operating savings has been established.

## Recommendation

Retain the four applications during assessment.

Evaluate shared property and lease services against mandatory office,
retail, entity-access and migration requirements.

Assess the two application pairs independently. Retain replacement
as an alternative if existing candidates cannot meet the requirements.

## Economic position

Under synthetic assumptions, 36-month modeled costs are:

| Option | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Retain and improve | EUR 244800 |
| Consolidate | EUR 242400 |
| Replace | EUR 313800 |

Consolidation's base advantage is EUR 2400, approximately 1 percent
of the retention option.

A 25 percent increase in one-time costs or a six-month transition delay
makes consolidation more expensive than retention.

The model supports further investigation rather than a cost-only
approval. Estimates are not vendor quotes or demonstrated savings.

## Evidence available

- Application and integration inventory with automated consistency checks.
- Explicit requirements and decision gates.
- Fourteen synthetic assessment records with unresolved questions.
- Reference rent calculator passing twelve predefined cases.
- Cost comparison with three sensitivity scenarios.
- Proposed migration stages and recovery boundaries.

The reference calculator does not establish candidate application fit.

## Conditions for a later investment decision

- Demonstrate mandatory functional requirements.
- Verify entity access restrictions.
- Reconcile a representative trial migration.
- Verify affected integration contracts and failure handling.
- Refine costs for the selected scope and candidates.
- Assign accountable owners and test recovery procedures.

## Discovery deliverables

Produce a requirements assessment, data profile, candidate comparison,
updated cost estimate and recommendation.

The executive sponsor should approve a timebox and spending limit
before discovery begins. Neither has been established in this portfolio.

## Stop or reconsider when

- Mandatory requirements cannot be met.
- Unresolved data or access risks prevent safe migration.
- Revised costs invalidate the investment rationale.
- Required recovery arrangements cannot be demonstrated.
